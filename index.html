❓ Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Quick answers to the most common questions about finding and hiring a caregiver on Carehia.

How do I find a caregiver near me? +
Enter your ZIP code on the home screen, select your care needs (elder care, dementia, post-surgery recovery, etc.), and Carehia instantly shows you verified caregivers in your area. No account required to browse. The whole process takes under 2 minutes.
Are caregivers on Carehia background checked? +
Yes. Every caregiver on Carehia goes through a national background check and identity verification before their profile goes live. Each profile also displays a transparent Trust Score (0–100) based on verification, reviews, response time, and experience — so you always know exactly who you're hiring.
How much does it cost to find a caregiver? +
Browsing caregivers is completely free — no credit card required. You can view profiles, trust scores, certifications, and reviews for free. Contact information is available through an affordable subscription plan, starting at $15/month — a fraction of what agencies charge.
How do caregivers get paid? +
Carehia is an independent caregiver marketplace. Caregivers set their own rates and get paid directly by families. Our built-in invoice generator and time tracker make it easy for caregivers to bill professionally and get paid on time.
How is Carehia different from Care.com? +
Care.com charges caregivers $20–40/month just to message a client. Carehia is free to join and only charges caregivers $4.99 per real booking they choose to unlock. We also give caregivers free professional tools (time tracker, invoicing, document vault, mileage tracking) that Care.com doesn't offer at all.
What types of care can I find on Carehia? +
Carehia covers 23 care specialties including: Elder Care, Dementia Care, Alzheimer's Support, Post-Surgery Recovery, Wheelchair Assistance, Medication Management, Bathing & Grooming, Meal Preparation, Companionship, Transportation, Overnight Care, Physical Therapy Aid, Wound Care, Hospice Support, Mental Health Support, Stroke Recovery, Disability Support, and more.
Can I join Carehia as a caregiver? +
Absolutely. Joining as a caregiver is free and takes under 60 seconds. Sign up with Google or email, set your ZIP code, pick your specialties, and your profile goes live immediately. You'll start receiving care requests from local families. You only pay ($4.99) when you want to unlock contact details for a real booking.
Is Carehia available in my city? +
Carehia is growing rapidly across the United States. We currently have active caregivers in major metro areas and are expanding daily. Enter your ZIP code to see available caregivers near you — if your area has limited coverage, joining as a caregiver helps grow the local network and you'll be first to receive requests.
