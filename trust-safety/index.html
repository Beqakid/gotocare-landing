Our Trust Approach

Carehia helps families and caregivers connect with more confidence through caregiver profiles, Trust Passport steps, reviews, reporting tools, and admin review processes.

Carehia's trust tools are designed to support better decisions, but they do not guarantee safety, qualifications, performance, or outcomes.

We are continuously improving our safety systems. Some features are in active beta and labeled clearly.

💼

Caregiver Code of Conduct

By using Carehia as a caregiver, you agree to:

  • Treat clients, families, and Carehia staff with respect
  • Arrive on time and communicate if delayed
  • Provide care only within your skills, training, and agreed duties
  • Keep client information private and confidential
  • Avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or unsafe behavior
  • Not misrepresent certifications, experience, background status, or identity
  • Not ask clients to move payments off-platform in a way that violates Carehia rules
  • Report safety concerns, incidents, or emergencies promptly
  • Follow reasonable and safe home rules
  • Maintain professional boundaries at all times

⚠️ Carehia may limit, suspend, or remove access if a caregiver violates these expectations or creates a safety concern.

🏠

Client Code of Conduct

By using Carehia to find care, you and your family agree to:

  • Treat caregivers with respect
  • Provide accurate care request details
  • Not request duties outside the agreed care scope
  • Provide a safe working environment
  • Not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or intimidate caregivers
  • Not ask caregivers to perform illegal, unsafe, or inappropriate tasks
  • Communicate schedule changes as early as possible
  • Pay agreed amounts according to Carehia rules
  • Report concerns through Carehia instead of retaliating
  • Respect caregiver privacy and professional boundaries

⚠️ Carehia may limit, suspend, or remove access if a client or family member violates these expectations or creates a safety concern.
